

\# SamvidhanSathi - Prompt Comparison



\## Objective



Compare Prompt Version 1 (Direct QA) and Prompt Version 2

(Structured Reasoning) using 10 Constitution-related questions.



\## Prompt Versions



\### Version 1

Direct question answering with simple language and

relevant constitutional Articles.



\### Version 2

Structured approach:

\- Identify topic

\- Identify relevant Article

\- Explain provision

\- Give example

\- Check information availability

| 1 | What is Article 14 of the Indian Constitution? | Explain equality before law |

| 9 | How can a citizen approach Supreme Court to protect Fundamental Rights under Article 32? | Explain constitutional remedies |

| 10 | What is the difference between Article 14 and Article 15? | Explain equality and non-discrimination |

\## Evaluation Criteria



1\. Accuracy: Is the answer constitutionally correct?

2\. Relevance: Does it answer the actual question?

3\. Simplicity: Is the explanation understandable?

4\. Completeness: Does it mention the relevant Article?

5\. Safety: Does it avoid inventing unavailable information?

6\. JSON Format: Are answer, confidence and follow\_up returned correctly?
